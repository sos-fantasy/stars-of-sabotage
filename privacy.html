    Privacy policy

    Stars of Sabotage is run by Sudden Works LLC. This page covers starsofsabotage.com and the Stars of Sabotage accounts on Instagram, Facebook, X and Pinterest.

    What the website collects

    Nothing that identifies you. The site has no sign-up, no accounts, no forms, no ads and no analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

    Our web host, Hostinger, keeps standard server logs when any page loads. A log line can include your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. We do not use those logs to track or profile visitors.

    The Video page shows thumbnails served by YouTube (i.ytimg.com), so loading that page sends your IP address to YouTube. The YouTube player loads only after you press play, from the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com domain. From that point YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

    How we post to social media

    A program we run posts our own score cards to the Stars of Sabotage accounts on Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest through each platform's official API. On Pinterest it creates Pins and boards on the Stars of Sabotage account and reads that account's own profile and board list.

    The program does not read, collect or store information about any other Pinterest, Instagram, Facebook or X user. It does not read your Pins, boards, followers or messages.

    The access tokens that let the program post are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets, which only the account owner can manage. They are used only to post to and manage our own accounts, and are never shared or sold.

    Sharing and selling

    We do not sell or share personal information.

    Children

    The site is a general audience sports stats site. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

    Changes

    If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
